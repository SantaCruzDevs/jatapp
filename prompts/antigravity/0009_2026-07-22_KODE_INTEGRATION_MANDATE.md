## Official Statement

The **Knowledge-Oriented Development Ecosystem (KODE)** is hereby officially adopted as the engineering methodology of Santa Cruz Devs.

KODE establishes the official governance framework for the development, evolution and preservation of software engineering knowledge across all current and future projects.

The methodology is designed as a human-centered collaborative ecosystem where people retain authority and specialized AI agents extend the capabilities of engineering teams.

# Approved Decisions

The following decisions are now considered official knowledge and shall become part of the methodology.

1. The methodology name is officially established as **KODE**.

2. KODE stands for **Knowledge-Oriented Development Ecosystem**.

3. The Constitution remains the highest governing document.

4. Knowledge is the primary strategic asset of every project.

5. Human beings retain all decision-making authority.

6. Specialized AI agents augment human capabilities but never replace human governance.

7. Knowledge Assets constitute the official mechanism for preserving organizational knowledge.

8. Statements (STM) officially declare approved organizational knowledge.

9. Prompts (PRM) execute approved Statements and shall never replace governance decisions.

---

# Implementation Authorization

The Implementation Engineer & Project Librarian is hereby authorized to perform the following activities.

1. Create the official KODE repository structure.

2. Integrate the Constitution as the highest governing document.

3. Incorporate the Foundational Declaration.

4. Register the official methodology identity.

5. Register the official guiding principle.

6. Update the documentation hierarchy.

7. Align all existing documentation with KODE terminology.

8. Preserve backward compatibility with existing project documentation.

9. Register every modification performed during the integration process.

10. Produce an Integration Report summarizing every implemented change.

---

# Implementation Restrictions

The Implementation Engineer shall not:

• modify constitutional principles

• reinterpret approved decisions

• introduce new governance rules

• summarize approved chapters

• remove approved knowledge

• alter the meaning of approved concepts

Editorial improvements, formatting, indexing and cross references are permitted provided that no approved content is modified.
