# KIM-0003 — Knowledge Governance Guide (KGG)

## Role

Implementation Engineer & Project Librarian

---

# Context

The KODE Constitution and the Knowledge Asset Catalog (KAC) have been officially integrated into the repository.

The methodology now requires a governance guide that explains how Knowledge Assets are created, reviewed, approved, maintained and evolved throughout the lifecycle of a project.

The Knowledge Governance Guide (KGG) shall become the operational governance manual of KODE.

It shall transform constitutional principles into practical governance rules while preserving the authority hierarchy established by the Constitution.

---

# Objective

Design and implement Version 1.0 Draft of the Knowledge Governance Guide (KGG).

The document shall define the governance processes that regulate the complete lifecycle of every Knowledge Asset recognized by KODE.

Its purpose is to ensure consistency, traceability, accountability and long-term maintainability across every project adopting KODE.

---

# Scope

The guide shall govern every Knowledge Asset defined in the Knowledge Asset Catalog.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Constitution
- Project Bible
- Statements (STM)
- Prompts (PRM)
- Architecture Decision Records (ADR)
- Knowledge Impact Analysis (KIA)
- Knowledge Debt (KD)
- Standards
- Templates
- Runbooks
- Sprint Documentation
- Source Code
- Changelog

---

Purpose of Knowledge Governance.

---

## 2. Governance Principles

Translate constitutional principles into operational governance.

## 3. Governance Roles

Define responsibilities for:

- Methodology Owner
- Product Owner
- Project Director
- Enterprise Architecture Reviewer
- Chief Technology Officer Reviewer

# Requirements

The guide shall:

remain fully aligned with the Constitution;

remain fully aligned with the KAC;

remain technology independent;

support human-centered governance;

support specialized AI collaboration;

remain reusable across projects;

avoid unnecessary bureaucracy;

favor practical engineering.

---

# Restrictions

Do not modify constitutional principles.

Do not redefine governance philosophy.

Do not assign authority to AI.

Do not duplicate KAC definitions.

Do not introduce vendor-specific concepts.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

# Deliverables

Implementation shall produce:

Knowledge Governance Guide Version 1.0 Draft

Updated repository references (if required)

Cross-reference validation

Changelog update

---

# Success Criteria

The Knowledge Governance Guide shall become the operational governance manual of KODE.

Every Knowledge Asset shall have clearly defined governance.

Every governance activity shall have an accountable human role.

Every AI role shall remain collaborative.

Every governance process shall preserve traceability.

The guide shall be internally consistent with both the Constitution and the Knowledge Asset Catalog.

---

# Final Instruction

This document is expected to become one of the core standards of KODE.

Design it as a long-term engineering standard intended to evolve through future versions while preserving backward compatibility with previously approved governance principles.
