# KIM-0002 — Knowledge Asset Catalog (KAC) Foundation

## Role

Implementation Engineer & Project Librarian

---

# Context

The KODE Constitution has been officially approved and integrated into the repository.

The methodology is now entering the standardization phase.

The next official Knowledge Asset to be created is the **Knowledge Asset Catalog (KAC)**.

The KAC shall become the authoritative reference describing every official Knowledge Asset recognized by KODE.

This document will establish the common vocabulary used throughout the methodology and all future projects.

The KAC shall be considered a governed document and must remain aligned with the KODE Constitution at all times.

---

# Objective

Design and implement the first official version of the **Knowledge Asset Catalog (KAC)**.

The document shall define every Knowledge Asset currently recognized by the methodology, establish its purpose, ownership, lifecycle, governance rules and relationships with other assets.

The objective is not merely to list document types, but to define the knowledge architecture of KODE.

---

# Scope

The first version of the KAC shall include, at minimum, the following Knowledge Assets:

* KA — Knowledge Asset
* KAC — Knowledge Asset Catalog
* STM — Statement
* PRM — Prompt
* ADR — Architecture Decision Record
* KD — Knowledge Debt
* KIA — Knowledge Impact Analysis
* Project Constitution
* Project Bible
* Runbooks
* Architecture Documents
* Standards
* Templates
* Changelog
* Sprint Documentation
* Source Code
* Repository Structure

Additional Knowledge Assets may be proposed if they improve consistency, but they shall be clearly identified as proposals and shall not become official without approval.

---

# Required Structure

The document shall contain at minimum:

1. Purpose of the Knowledge Asset Catalog.

2. Relationship with the Constitution.

3. Definition of a Knowledge Asset.

4. Knowledge Asset Classification.

5. Official catalog of Knowledge Assets.

6. Identifier conventions.

7. Ownership model.

8. Governance responsibilities.

9. Lifecycle of Knowledge Assets.

10. Versioning policy.

11. Traceability requirements.

12. Cross-reference rules.

13. Deprecation process.

14. Archival process.

15. Future extensibility.

---

# Requirements

The document shall:

* follow the governance principles defined in the Constitution;
* maintain a human-centered governance model;
* preserve traceability;
* define responsibilities without assigning authority to AI;
* remain technology-independent;
* be reusable across projects;
* avoid vendor-specific terminology;
* distinguish mandatory assets from optional assets;
* define relationships among assets.

---

# Restrictions

Do not modify the Constitution.

Do not redefine constitutional principles.

Do not invent governance outside the Constitution.

Do not create redundant Knowledge Assets.

Do not introduce technology-specific assumptions.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

Favor clarity, consistency and long-term maintainability.

---

# Deliverables

The implementation shall produce:

* Knowledge Asset Catalog (Version 1.0 Draft)
* Updated repository references (if required)
* Cross-reference map
* Changelog entry documenting the addition

---

# Success Criteria

The KAC shall become the official reference for every Knowledge Asset recognized by KODE.

Every asset shall have:

* a clear purpose;
* an owner;
* a lifecycle;
* governance responsibilities;
* naming conventions;
* relationships with other Knowledge Assets.

The document shall be internally consistent with the Constitution and ready for future review by the Enterprise Architecture Reviewer and the Chief Technology Officer Reviewer.

---

End of Mandate.
