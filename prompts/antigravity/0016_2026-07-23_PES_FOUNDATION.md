# PES-001 — Prompt Engineering Standard

## Role

Implementation Engineer & Project Librarian

---

# Context

KODE recognizes Prompts as official Knowledge Assets (PRM) that encapsulate reusable engineering knowledge, operational instructions and specialized interactions with agents.

Unlike traditional prompt engineering practices, KODE treats Prompts as governed engineering artifacts subject to version control, traceability, ownership, maintenance and continuous evolution.

This standard establishes the engineering principles governing the complete lifecycle of Prompts within projects adopting KODE.

---

# Objective

Design and implement Version 1.0 of the **Prompt Engineering Standard (PES-001).**

The standard shall define how Prompts are created, classified, governed, maintained, versioned and retired as official Knowledge Assets.

The document shall remain vendor-neutral and technology-independent.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to every Prompt officially maintained within KODE.

Including, but not limited to:

- Integration Mandates (KIM)
- Operational Prompts
- Review Prompts
- Documentation Prompts
- Architecture Prompts
- Analysis Prompts
- Validation Prompts
- Automation Prompts
- Specialized Agent Instructions
- Future Prompt Types

# Success Criteria

Prompt Engineering shall become an officially governed engineering discipline within KODE.

Every Prompt shall be managed as a Knowledge Asset.

Prompt lifecycle shall be fully defined.

Prompt Debt shall become an official governance concept.

Prompt relationships with Statements, Knowledge Assets and Specialized Agents shall be formally documented.

The standard shall remain technology-independent while supporting future generations of AI and automation systems.

---

# Final Instruction

Design this document as a long-term engineering standard.

The objective is not to teach prompting techniques.

The objective is to establish Prompt Engineering as a governed knowledge discipline fully integrated into the KODE ecosystem.
