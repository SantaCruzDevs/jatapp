# JAT-DEMO-0001 — Commercial Demo Roadmap

## Objective

The current priority is NOT the production product.

The current objective is to build a polished commercial demonstration that can be presented to prospective clients.

This demo will later become the functional reference for the real product, but for now its purpose is sales and validation.

---

# Goals

The demo must:

- Look professional.
- Feel fast.
- Showcase the complete business workflow.
- Use realistic sample data.
- Demonstrate the value proposition in under 10 minutes.

---

# Deliverable

Create:

docs/

DEMO/

COMMERCIAL_DEMO_ROADMAP.md

---

The roadmap shall identify:

• Critical features required for the demo.

• Features that may be simulated.

• Features that can be postponed.

• Demo script for customer presentations.

• Recommended sample data.

• Visual polish priorities.

• Risk assessment.

• Estimated implementation order.

---

# Classification

Every feature shall be classified as:

Demo Critical

Demo Important

Optional

Production Only

---

# Constraints

Do NOT redesign the product.

Do NOT add enterprise features that are unnecessary for the demonstration.

Prioritize impact over completeness.

The objective is to maximize customer confidence during presentations.
