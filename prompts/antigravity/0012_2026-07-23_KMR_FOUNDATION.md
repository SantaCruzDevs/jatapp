# KIM-0004 — KODE Methodology Register (KMR)

## Role

Implementation Engineer & Project Librarian

---

# Context

During the implementation of KODE Core, it was identified that a significant amount of officially approved methodological knowledge exists only within collaborative design discussions and has not yet been institutionalized as an official Knowledge Asset.

This situation contradicts one of the fundamental principles of KODE:

> Knowledge is the primary strategic asset of the project.

Therefore, the methodology requires a dedicated Knowledge Asset responsible for preserving its institutional identity, official status, historical evolution and governing metadata.

This document shall become the official institutional record of the methodology itself.

---

# Objective

Design and implement Version 1.0 Draft of the **KODE Methodology Register (KMR)**.

The KMR shall become the authoritative source describing the official identity, ownership, evolution and governance status of KODE.

The document shall preserve institutional knowledge that does not belong to the Constitution, the Knowledge Asset Catalog or the Knowledge Governance Guide.

---

# Scope

The KMR shall document the methodology itself.

It shall not redefine constitutional principles.

It shall not duplicate governance rules.

It shall complement existing Knowledge Assets.

# Requirements

The KMR shall:

preserve institutional knowledge;

avoid duplicating constitutional content;

remain technology independent;

remain aligned with the Constitution;

remain aligned with the KAC;

remain aligned with the KGG;

be reusable across projects;

become the official identity document of KODE.

---

# Restrictions

Do not modify the Constitution.

Do not redefine principles.

Do not duplicate governance.

Do not duplicate KAC definitions.

Do not introduce vendor-specific information.

Do not change approved terminology.

---

# Deliverables

Implementation shall produce:

KODE Methodology Register Version 1.0 Draft

Repository references update (if required)

Cross-reference validation

Changelog update

---

# Success Criteria

The Methodology Register shall become the authoritative institutional record of KODE.

Every officially approved element defining the methodology shall exist in an official Knowledge Asset.

No strategic methodological knowledge shall remain exclusively within conversations.

The document shall preserve the identity, ownership, history and evolution of KODE while remaining fully consistent with the Constitution, the KAC and the KGG.

---

# Final Instruction

The KODE Methodology Register is not a governance document.

It is the institutional memory of the methodology itself.

Design it as a permanent Knowledge Asset intended to preserve the identity and evolution of KODE throughout future versions.
